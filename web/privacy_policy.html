Privacy Policy for Darb (درب)

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

Welcome to Darb ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data processed while using our app. Data collection is strictly limited to facilitating campus navigation features and collecting anonymous usage analytics to improve our services.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Precise Location Data

  • Data Type: Precise geographical location coordinates (GPS).
  • Purpose: Location data is used exclusively for core App Functionality—specifically to display your real-time position on the campus map and calculate foot or driving routes to campus locations.
  • Nature of Processing (Ephemeral): Location data is processed temporarily in-memory on your device for immediate route rendering. Location coordinates are not saved in remote databases, logged to personal profiles, or tracked persistently.

B. App Activity and Usage Analytics

  • Data Type: Anonymous user interaction events (e.g., app launches, campus search terms, route choices).
  • Service Provider: We use Aptabase, a privacy-first, open-source analytics platform designed to safeguard user identity.
  • Purpose: To analyze statistical search queries, identify missing campus map items, and enhance application performance.
  • Storage: All analytics data is collected entirely anonymously without tracking cookies or user profile creation.

2. Third-Party Services and Data Transfers

  • Route Calculation (OSRM): When requesting a route, start and target coordinates are sent to an Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM) server to generate map polyline coordinates. These requests contain no personal identifiers.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share your data or location coordinates with advertising networks or marketing third parties.

3. Account Creation & Data Security

  • No Accounts Required: Darb does not require account registration or login.
  • Encryption in Transit: All network communication (including map tiles, routing queries, and analytics telemetry) is encrypted in transit via standard secure protocols (HTTPS/TLS).
