# Privacy Policy for SQU Darb

**Last Updated:** November 9, 2026

Welcome to **SQU Darb** ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data collected when you use our application. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your choices regarding your data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

### A. Precise Location Data
* **Data Collected:** Precise geographical location coordinates (GPS).
* **Purpose:** Location data is used strictly for core **App Functionality**—specifically to display your real-time position on the campus map and calculate navigation/walking routes to selected university buildings.
* **Storage & Retention (Ephemeral):** Location data is processed temporarily in memory on your device to calculate routes and map positions. It is **not** stored permanently on remote servers, logged to analytics profiles, or linked to your personal identity.

### B. App Activity and Usage Analytics
* **Data Collected:** Anonymous app interaction events (e.g., app launches, search query terms, selected route modes).
* **Provider:** We utilize **Aptabase**, a privacy-first, open-source telemetry service.
* **Purpose:** Collected anonymously for **Analytics** to help us improve campus map search results, identify unmapped search terms, and optimize app performance.
* **Storage:** Data is logged anonymously without relying on tracking cookies or persistent personal user accounts.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & External Requests

* **Routing Engine (OSRM):** When you request navigation instructions, anonymized start and destination coordinates are sent to an Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM) server to construct polyline route geometry.
* **Third-Party Data Sharing:** We **do not sell, rent, or share** your personal data or location coordinates with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing partners.

---

## 3. Account Creation & Data Security

* **No Accounts Required:** You do not need to create an account or sign in to use Darb.
* **Data Encryption in Transit:** All network requests—including analytics logs, map tiles, and routing calculations—are transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS encryption.

---

## 4. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

Although Darb does not maintain user profiles, you have the right to request the deletion of any identifiable support records or logged interaction data.

* **Data Deletion Request Link:** You may submit a data deletion request at any time using our official [Data Deletion Request Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdpNKauoZuEppN_EOyCFj-CR5upQXHjjapyIoTYESceXHT8gQ/viewform?usp=publish-editor).
